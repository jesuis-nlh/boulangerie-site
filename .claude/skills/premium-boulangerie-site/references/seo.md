# Local SEO — Lyon bakery

Target intents (map one primary per page, don't stuff):
- Home: "boulangerie [quartier] Lyon", "boulangerie au levain Lyon"
- /produits/: "pain au levain Lyon", "croissant Lyon", "brioche aux pralines Lyon"
- /sur-mesure/: "gâteau d'anniversaire Lyon [arrondissement]", "pâtisserie sur commande Lyon"
- /commander/: "click and collect boulangerie Lyon"

On-page: neighbourhood + arrondissement in H1/lede only if true; product names as they appear on the shelf; alt text describes the item, not keywords.

Off-page (client tasks, document in handover): Google Business Profile (same NAP as `config.contact`, categories "Boulangerie" + "Pâtisserie", hours incl. holidays, real photos), Apple Business Connect, PagesJaunes, OpenStreetMap shop tag. Ask happy customers for reviews — never write or buy them.

Structured data: `Bakery` (LocalBusiness subtype) site-wide; `Product` + `Offer` on product pages; no `Review`/`AggregateRating` without a real source. Validate with Google's Rich Results Test before launch.
